Four moments at the gym. Real screens.

Every image comes from today's web app, with sample data.

Bench is taken. Now what?

Tap "Machine in use": 3in suggests exercises for the same target muscle, including other equipment. The swap is for today only; your plan stays the same.

Last set. The timer starts.

Tap the flame next to a set and a 90-second timer starts for your set to failure. Add a song of your choice if you like.

Week changed? Drag the day.

Your plan shows training days, sets and the muscles you worked. To swap two days, drag one onto the other; the monthly calendar works the same way.

Every set done? The weight goes up.

The rule-based coach looks at your last session and suggests the next load, with the reason written next to it. You can always change the numbers. Turn it on with your consent in Settings.
